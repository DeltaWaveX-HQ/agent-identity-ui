1Our product has three parts

Think of an office building. Every visitor needs a pass, a guard checks the pass at the door, and a smart camera watches for anyone acting strangely.

🪪
ID passEvery agent gets its own pass (an OAuth 2.1 token) saying who sent it and exactly what it may do — only what the user agreed to.
💂
Security guardA gateway that sits between the agent and the internet. Every request must pass through it, and anything not on the pass is blocked.
🧠
Smart risk checkA model that scores each agent: where it came from, what it's asking for, and how it's behaving. Risky agents are stopped or a human is asked first.

Spotlight: Microsoft

Yes — Microsoft does detect risky agents now. Here's what it does and doesn't do.

  • ✅ Gives every new Copilot Studio agent its own ID automatically.
  • ✅ Flags risky agents (strange sign-ins, touching unusual files, snooping in the directory) and can block them.
  • ⚠️ The risk check runs after the fact, not live — Microsoft's own docs say every agent risk detection is offline.
  • ⚠️ When an agent acts for a user, its risk is blamed on the user, not the agent.
  • ⚠️ Live blocking of agent actions is mostly still in preview, and works best inside Microsoft's own world.
  • 📅 Its big yearly event (Ignite) is in November, with a session on "your riskiest agents" — expect more.

5How we can still win

Giant companies already sell the ID pass, often free inside their plans. So the pass alone can't be our product. Here is where the gaps are — our hypotheses, to test with real customers.

⚡
Live risk check, not after the factMicrosoft's risk detection is offline, and most others are still on the roadmap. Ours scores every action as it happens and stops it before damage.
🔗
Follow the whole chainAnalysts say no vendor fully tracks permission as it passes from user → agent → sub-agent → tool. That's exactly our OAuth consent + delegation idea.
🌍
Works with every platformBig players work best inside their own cloud. Companies use agents from many vendors and want one neutral control — Gartner says the same.
📏
Prove it worksNo vendor has published independent proof of how well its agent detection works. Publishing honest test results would make us stand out.
🇮🇳
Start in IndiaCERT-In is already asking for exactly these controls, and no Indian company focuses on this. Banks, fintech and payments are a natural first market.
⏱️
Move fast, aim to partnerBig companies are buying startups like this. A narrow, proven product can become a partner — or an acquisition — rather than a head-on rival.
